About

I am a master's student in Structural Engineering at Sharif University of Technology, with a bachelor's background in Aerospace Engineering. My work sits at the intersection of classical structural mechanics and modern machine intelligence — combining finite element modeling of composite materials with data-driven methods for structural health monitoring. I am especially interested in how agentic AI and large language models can accelerate engineering design and turn sensor data into actionable digital twins. Through research on inverse finite element methods, piezoelectric sensing, and multi-agent design systems, I aim to bridge the gap between physics-based simulation and intelligent automation.
