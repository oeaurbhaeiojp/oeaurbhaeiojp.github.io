About

I'm a structural engineer working at the intersection of agentic AI and structural engineering, building systems where intelligent agents understand engineering problems, retrieve domain knowledge, and produce design solutions autonomously. I researched and developed a multi-agent LLM framework using CrewAI and RAG pipelines that lets engineers describe mechanical linkage requirements in natural language and receive synthesized designs. In my M.Sc. thesis, I am currently exploring where agentic AI can create the most impact within structural engineering, an area I am deeply interested in and see as one of the most promising frontiers in the field.

On the structural side, my bachelor thesis developed and validated a piezoelectric sensor framework for early-stage crack detection in metallic plates using wave propagation simulations in Abaqus. I also researched and built an iFEM-based system integrating strain data with acoustic emission signals for real-time structural deformation reconstruction and damage localization. I'm interested in connecting with researchers, academics, and engineers working at the intersection of AI and structural engineering. Always open to exchanging ideas and exploring collaborations.
