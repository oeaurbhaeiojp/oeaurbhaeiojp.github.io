About

I'm a structural engineer interested in how agentic AI can support structural engineering work. Mostly I want to know whether an AI system can understand an engineering problem on its own, bring in the domain knowledge it needs, and help produce a design. The applications I care about most are structural health monitoring, early damage detection and real-time deformation analysis. I think bringing AI into structural engineering could change a good deal of the field's day-to-day work, and I'd like to connect with researchers and engineers working on similar problems.
