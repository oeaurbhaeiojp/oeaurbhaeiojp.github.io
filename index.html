About

I'm an engineer who enjoys solving problems. During my B.Sc. in aerospace engineering, I looked across its different fields, and structures was the one I ended up caring about most; that interest took me straight into an M.Sc. in structural engineering as a talented-student direct-entry candidate, and my B.Sc. thesis was on structural health monitoring. I started learning about AI and its methods back in my undergraduate years, and now, in my M.Sc., I work on composite material design using agentic AI, which covers both of the fields I care about: structures and AI. I want to continue into further graduate study, staying on structure-related problems and solving them with new methods. I'd like to do my PhD in a lab or research environment that works close to real-world problems and has a dynamic setting.
